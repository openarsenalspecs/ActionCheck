# Notice for ActionCheck

**ActionCheck** – *Every Action Logged. Every Deviation Noted.*

This repository contains the source code for **ActionCheck**, an open-source platform designed to track politicians’ promises, votes, financial activity, and potential conflicts of interest. It is intended to empower citizens with transparency and accountability tools.

---

## Key Points

- All data and features are for **public interest and civic engagement**.
- The project is **open-source under the GNU Affero General Public License Version 3+ (AGPL‑3.0+)**. This means any use of the software, including over a network, requires sharing modifications under the same license.
- Users and contributors are encouraged to **verify all data sources**. The repository is a tool for transparency; it does not guarantee complete accuracy of third-party data.
- **Attribution Required:** All contributions and forks must credit **Roxanne Ardary** and [roxanneardary.com](https://www.roxanneardary.com/) under section 7 of AGPL‑3.0+, where applicable.

---

## Usage Guidelines

- You may use, modify, and redistribute this software under the terms of the AGPL‑3.0+.  
- Contributions should follow community-driven transparency standards and maintain accurate referencing for political promises, votes, financial filings, and corporate board memberships.  
- All new data sources or modifications should be documented clearly in the repository to maintain auditability.
